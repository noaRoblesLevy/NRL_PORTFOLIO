About

I'm a third-year student in Applied Computer Science (Infrastructure & Security) at Karel de Grote University in Antwerp, and I start a Bachelor of Mathematics at KU Leuven in 2027. Alongside my studies I work as an AI & transformation consultant at BEEGO, where the automations I build run in production for Belgium's largest logistics company.

I learn by building. My projects range from a field-operations platform used by a real client, to a NetFlow-based anomaly detector, an OSINT risk platform, a redundant dual-stack enterprise network and a self-built drone flight controller. On the side I do quantitative research on crypto derivatives, where I've learned that most ideas fail and that rigorous out-of-sample testing is what separates an edge from noise.

Outside of tech: chess, poker and cybersecurity, all games of incomplete information.

Research 2026

Crypto Funding-Carry Research

A Python research harness for finding trading edges that survive real costs. After rejecting more than a dozen price-prediction ideas out of sample, it found a market-neutral strategy that harvests the funding-rate spread across 23 perpetual futures.

  • Long lowest-funding, short highest-funding coins with a rolling BTC beta hedge and volatility targeting
  • Backtested Sharpe 1.34 (2023 to 2025) and 1.60 on held-out 2025 data, validated across two exchanges
  • Monte Carlo pass-rate analysis against prop-firm drawdown rules, plus an automated forward test
